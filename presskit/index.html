About the game

Ardhan is a roguelike deckbuilder where every fight happens on a board. You play minions, spells and a hero power from a mana curve, trade blows with the rival's minions and try to bring their hero down. Between fights you pick a path through branching maps, take rewards, visit the Caravan and face a boss at the end of each act. The rival's next cards and attacks are shown before they happen, so you can plan around them.

The game is made by one person. It plays offline; the Arena, where you face decks other players have built, is optional.

New in 1.0.4: living cards

Every card picture can now act out a move. The Patrol Guard plants his halberd and lowers it toward you. The Ur-Wolf lunges out of its frame. Players bring a card they own to life with gold in the Collection, from 150 to 2000 gold by rarity. A living card moves in the hand and in the Collection and stays still once it is played on the board, so battles stay easy to read. Each card can be set to Living or Still, and one switch in Settings turns them all off. It is cosmetic only: stats stay the same.

The update also puts all gold into one purse shared by every game and fixes two crashes.
