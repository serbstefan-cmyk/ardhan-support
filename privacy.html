Ardhan — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

1. Who I am

Ardhan is a single-player card game made and published by Ștefan Mihai Șerb, a private individual living in Romania ("I", "me"). For the small amount of personal data described in this policy, I am the data controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

  • E-mail: contact@unitechdigital.net

2. The short version

  • There is no account, no login, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no crash reporting in the game.
  • Your progress is saved on your device. The game has no cloud save of its own (for Steam Cloud, see section 4).
  • The game goes online only to download optional content from GitHub, to check for updates of content you already downloaded, and, on Android only, to talk to Google Play for purchases.
  • The game sends me nothing. I only receive what you send me yourself (for example an e-mail) and the sales reports the stores give me.

3. Where this policy applies

This policy covers the game Ardhan everywhere I publish it:

  • Android, through Google Play;
  • iPhone and iPad, through the App Store;
  • Windows and Linux, through Steam;
  • Windows, through the Microsoft Store.

The same rules apply on every platform. The few differences are named below.

4. What the game keeps on your device

The game stores these things on your device only:

  • Your save: runs in progress, your collection, shards and gold, unlocked items, achievements, statistics, run history and your settings (language, volume and similar choices).
  • On Android only: a list of Google Play purchase tokens that were already delivered, so that a purchase is never granted twice.
  • Optional packs you downloaded (see section 5).
  • Share cards you saved: a picture of a finished run with the faction, the result, the act reached, the run's seed and one line of text. It contains nothing about you or your device. On Android the game can pass it to the system share menu, and you choose the app. On other platforms it is saved in the game's own folder.
  • On Windows and Linux: a technical log file (logs/godot.log) in the same folder as the save.

None of this is sent to me or to anyone else by the game.

Clipboard. The game puts text on your clipboard only when you press a button: the backup code of your save (Settings, "Copy save code"), the run's seed text (the "Copy" and "Save share card" buttons after a run) and my e-mail address (the contact buttons). Where you paste it is your choice. The game reads the clipboard only when you press "Import save code" and confirm, or "Paste" when you enter a seed.

Steam Cloud (Steam only). The game itself has no cloud save. If Steam Cloud is switched on for Ardhan and in your Steam settings, Steam keeps a copy of the save folder in your Steam account. Valve runs that service under its own privacy policy (section 6); I cannot see those copies. You can switch Steam Cloud off in Steam.

Deleting your data. On Android, iPhone and iPad, uninstalling the game deletes all of it. For the Microsoft Store version, Windows normally removes the game's data when you uninstall it. On Windows and Linux through Steam, the save folder stays on your computer after you uninstall; you can delete it yourself (a Steam Cloud copy, if any, is managed in Steam):

  • Windows: %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Ardhan
  • Linux: ~/.local/share/Godot/app_userdata/Ardhan

5. When the game uses the internet

The game works offline. It connects to the internet only in the cases below. None of them goes to a server of mine.

5.1 Optional downloads from GitHub

Some content is an optional download:

  • On Android: the card art for most cards, the narration in languages other than English, and the story cinematics.
  • On iPhone, iPad, Windows and Linux: all card art and all nine narration languages are already inside the game. The only optional download there is the boss cinematics (about 5 MB).

A download starts only when you tap its Download button (on the title screen, the language screen, the Collection, the faction choice or in Settings; on Android the first start also asks you once whether to download the card art now). The files come over HTTPS from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com), a file-hosting service run by GitHub. Before a download, the game fetches a small list of the available packs (a "manifest") to learn their sizes and checksums, and it checks each download against that list before installing it.

5.2 Update checks for packs you already have

Only if you have installed a pack, the game also checks whether a newer version of it exists:

  • when you open Settings, the Collection or the faction choice, it may fetch the small manifest;
  • when the game starts, it checks the narration and cinematics packs you installed and, if a newer version exists, downloads it in the background without asking again.

It never starts a download of a pack you did not choose. If you have never installed a pack, the game makes none of these requests. You can avoid them at any time by playing offline.

5.3 Google Play purchases (Android only)

The Android version contains Google's Play Billing library. When the title screen opens, the game asks Google Play, through the Play Store app on your device, for the current prices of its products and for the purchases you made in this game, so that anything you paid for is delivered. When you buy something, Google Play handles the whole payment. The iPhone, iPad, Windows and Linux versions make no purchase requests at all.

5.4 What these connections carry

In every case the game sends no name, e-mail address, account, advertising identifier, location, contact list or any identifier made by me. GitHub and Google receive what any internet request carries: your IP address, the time, the file or service asked for, and basic technical information about the app and the device. On Android, Google's Play Billing library inside the game may also send Google technical information about how the purchase features work. GitHub handles this under the GitHub General Privacy Statement, and Google under the Google Privacy Policy. I do not receive your IP address or any of these details.

6. Purchases and the stores

  • Google Play (Android): the game offers optional in-app purchases. Google processes the payment. I never see your card or bank details. Google gives the game only the details of the purchase (such as the product, an order number and a purchase token), and the game unlocks the item in your save on your device.
  • App Store, Steam and Microsoft Store: the game sells nothing inside the game. If the game itself has a price on one of these stores, the store handles that purchase.
  • Each store keeps its own record of what you bought, and the store app on your device (Play Store, App Store, Steam, Microsoft Store) may collect data about how you use it, including play time. That is covered by the store's own policy, not by this one: Google, Apple, Valve (Steam), Microsoft.
  • Refunds are requested from the store where you paid.

7. Personal data I do receive

  • Messages you send me. The game's contact buttons copy my address, and one of them also opens your own e-mail app; nothing is sent until you send it yourself. If you write to me, I receive your e-mail address, your name if your e-mail shows it, what you write and anything you attach (for example a screenshot, a save backup code or a log file). I use it only to answer you and to fix problems in the game.
  • Store reports. Google, Apple, Valve and Microsoft give me sales and payout reports and order details (such as an order number, the product, the price, the date and the country). Some store tools let me look up a single order, for example when you ask me for help with it. I use this for support, refunds, and my accounting and tax records.
  • Store statistics. The stores show me statistics they collect under their own policies, such as install counts, ratings and reviews, and crash or performance reports from people who agreed to share them with developers. I use them to improve the game. I do not try to identify anyone from them.

I do not sell personal data, I do not use it for advertising, and I make no automated decisions or profiles about anyone.

8. Why this is allowed (legal bases under the GDPR)

  • Answering your messages: my legitimate interest in supporting the people who play the game (Article 6(1)(f)); for a question about a purchase, also the performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b)).
  • Keeping store reports for accounting and tax: a legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)).
  • Making the downloads you ask for: providing the content you requested (Article 6(1)(b)).
  • Update checks for packs you installed: my legitimate interest in keeping the content you chose complete and working (Article 6(1)(f)). You can object, or simply play offline.

9. How long I keep it

  • E-mails: until your question is settled, and then no longer than 24 months after our last message, unless a message must be kept longer for a legal claim or a tax record (for example about a purchase).
  • Store reports: as long as Romanian tax and accounting law requires.
  • Everything on your device: until you delete it (see section 4).

10. Who else is involved

I do not share personal data with anyone, except that my e-mail provider stores my e-mails for me. The stores and GitHub act on their own account, under their own privacy policies (sections 5 and 6). Some of them, and possibly my e-mail provider, are based in, or process data in, the United States; their policies explain how they protect data sent outside the European Union.

11. Permissions and backups

Android permissions. INTERNET (downloads and Google Play), ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (to see whether the device is online), com.android.vending.BILLING (Google Play purchases), and one technical permission that Android creates for the app itself (…DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION), which gives access to nothing of yours. None of them lets the game read your contacts, location, photos, files, camera or microphone. On iPhone and iPad the game never asks for any of these either.

Backups. The Android version switches off Android's automatic backup (android:allowBackup is false), so Android does not copy your save to your Google account's backup. When you move to a new phone by cable or direct transfer, Android may still copy it. On iPhone and iPad, your device backup (iCloud or computer), if you use one, may include the game's data; Apple and you control that backup. On Windows and Linux, any backup is your own. To keep your progress safe, use the backup code in Settings.

12. Children

Ardhan is a fantasy card game with fantasy combat, made for players aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13. The game collects no personal data from anyone, children included. Please do not send me personal data about a child. If you are a parent and believe your child sent me personal data, write to me and I will delete it.

13. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to ask for access to your personal data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive it in a portable form. To use any of these rights, write to contact@unitechdigital.net. I will answer within one month. I may ask you to write from the same e-mail address, so that I know the request is yours.

Most of what the game knows about you never leaves your device, so I cannot see, export or delete it for you. You can delete it yourself (section 4).

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In Romania this is the National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing (ANSPDCP), 28-30 G-ral Gheorghe Magheru Blvd., District 1, 010336 Bucharest, e-mail anspdcp@dataprotection.ro, www.dataprotection.ro. You can also complain to the authority of the EU country where you live or work.

14. Changes to this policy

If the game's handling of data changes (for example a cloud save, an online mode, analytics, advertising, or purchases on another store), I will update this page before that version is released, change the "Last updated" date at the top, and mention the change in the game's notes. The address of this page stays the same.
